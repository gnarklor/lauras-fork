Transfer options from SNHU for a bachelor's in disinformation, journalism, policy and technology, without moving away from the Keene area. Checked November 19, 2026. Each card rates eight factors: green means a good fit, yellow means workable with tradeoffs, red means a problem.

Good fit Tradeoffs Problem

What you love

Disinformation and influence operations, journalism, local policy, ethics, history, language, AI and tools.

What you are good at

Research, interviewing, writing and conversation, and turning data into visuals for different audiences. Fifteen years of explaining technical products.

What the world needs

People who catch manipulative narratives as they spread, especially in local news and for working people.

What can pay

OSINT and research analyst, trust and safety, data journalism and civic tech roles (see the Roles tab; target $70K+).

Recommended path

Main route: transfer to UMass Amherst University Without Walls and design a concentration in Information Integrity and Democratic Communication. Add one in-person course a year for community: UMass PUBPOL/COMM 208 in Amherst, or a Keene State journalism, persuasion or genocide-studies course taken as transfer credit (get it approved first).

  1. Now: request your SNHU transcript, book a UWW pre-admission advising call, and ask HR about your tuition reimbursement cap and approved schools.
  2. First term: UWW 305 (degree plan with your faculty advisor) plus one Experiential Reflections course (Technology runs in fall, Public Policy in spring).
  3. Second term: UWW 370, where you write the portfolio that can earn credit for 15 years of technical sales and project work.
  4. Every term after: one or two concentration courses. Use DisinfoVault or your NH policy work as an independent study or practicum.
  5. After the BA: the UT Information Security and Integrity MS remains a fit (any major, 2.7 GPA).

Plan B: if in-person community matters more than flexibility, matriculate part-time at Keene State in Multimedia Journalism or Social Media with a Holocaust and Genocide Studies minor.
